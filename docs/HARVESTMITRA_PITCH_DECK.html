⚠️ THE NATIONAL PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Systemic Crisis in Indian Agriculture

Smallholder farmers in India lose up to 40% of their annual revenue due to information asymmetry, cartel middlemen, and fragmented transport.

₹50,000 Cr+
Annual Post-Harvest Loss
Perishable crops like tomatoes and onions spoil in transit due to uncoordinated transport and lack of cold storage dispatch.
35% - 50%
Middlemen Commission Drain
Unregulated commission agents (arhatiyas) take huge cuts, leaving the farmer with less than half the consumer retail price.
Zero Visibility
Inter-Mandi Price Gaps
Nearby mandis just 50km away may offer ₹800/quintal more, but farmers lack real-time transparent comparisons.
Digital Divide
Language & Tech Barriers
Complex English apps fail rural adoption. Farmers need instant vernacular voice support in Marathi, Hindi, and Gujarati.
