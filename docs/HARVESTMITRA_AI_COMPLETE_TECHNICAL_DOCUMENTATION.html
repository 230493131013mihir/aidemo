1. Executive Summary & Problem Solved

HarvestMitra AI is an integrated full-stack agricultural operating platform engineered to eliminate post-harvest losses and predatory intermediary margins for Indian farmers. It addresses four systemic rural vulnerabilities:

  • APMC Price Opacity: Local traders exploit price ignorance; HarvestMitra provides real-time mandi prices and moving averages across 50 regional mandis.
  • Exorbitant Transport Costs: Solo truck rental claims 30% of gross crop value; HarvestMitra's transport pooling engine enables 45%–63% cost savings.
  • Weather Uncertainty: Rain risk damages harvested crops; HarvestMitra integrates Open-Meteo 5-day forecasts with automated harvest advisories.
  • Subsidy Unawareness: Farmers miss government schemes; HarvestMitra automatically matches farmer land and crop profiles to 10 Central and State programs.

4. Core Modules & Mathematical Implementations

4.1 Harvest Decision Simulator

Located in backend/services/mathEngine.js and frontend/src/pages/HarvestPage.jsx.

  • Gross Revenue: Quantity (kg) × Price per kg (₹)
  • Total Cost: Transport + Packaging + Loading Labor + Mandi Cess (1.5%) + Holding Spoilage
  • Net Profit: Gross Revenue - Total Cost
  • Three-Way Evaluation:
    1. Option 1 (Sell Today): Local mandi, minimum risk, baseline revenue.
    2. Option 2 (Hold & Sell Later): Cold storage fees + 5% spoilage buffer evaluated against forecasted post-monsoon price surges.
    3. Option 3 (Transport to Terminal Mandi): Exploits distant mandi price arbitrage via shared freight pooling.

4.2 Shared Transport & Cargo Pooling

Located in backend/services/transportMatcher.js and frontend/src/pages/TransportPage.jsx.

  • Proportional Cost Formula: Farmer Cost = Total Freight × (Farmer Load kg / Pooled Load kg)
  • Cross-Contamination Safeguard: Prohibits incompatible crops (e.g., pungent onions with table tomatoes).
  • Digital APMC Gate Pass: Generates QR-verified cargo passes for checkpoint security inspectors.

4.3 Government Schemes & Eligibility Engine

Located in backend/services/schemeService.js and frontend/src/pages/SchemesPage.jsx.

  • Includes 10 active programs: PM-KISAN, PMFBY (Crop Insurance), PMKSY (Micro-Irrigation), i-Khedut Gujarat, Nanaji Deshmukh Krishi Sanjivani.
  • Automated rule matching: Evaluates farmer acreage, irrigation type, and crop against eligibility criteria.

4.4 Mitra AI Assistant

Located in backend/services/ai/aiService.js and frontend/src/pages/ChatPage.jsx.

  • Context enrichment: Automatically injects farmer land acreage, live mandi prices, and today's weather into prompt.
  • Full fallback safety: Guarantees plain-language responses in Marathi, Hindi, Gujarati, or English even with zero internet connectivity.
